> 🏆 1st in our track at the Gies AI Hackathon.
>
> But if we knew what the night ahead looked like, we would've prepared differently. None of us slept the night before. Few hours in, we were already running on empty. Rotating micro-naps in Grainger. Learning Microsoft Copilot Studio from scratch while trying to architect the orchestration logic for a 5-agent system at the same time.
>
> There were moments we genuinely didn't know if it would come together. Somehow, we found a way. Nobody complained. Nobody checked out. Every time one of us hit a wall, someone else picked it up. Shoutout to our powerhouse of a team: Luke Manthuruthil, Kelly Cong, & Mitchell Crevier ⚡️
>
> Here's what we built: **HireFlow.** A multi-agent hiring evaluation system that eliminates scoring drift. The hiring manager defines a custom rubric. The agent scores the resume and CV, flags strengths and gaps against specific levels of responsibility, and confirms each score. After interviews, it pulls the transcript, scores the candidate against an ideal candidate profile rooted in historical candidate admission data, and aggregates every round into one decision, being conscious how many are let through at every round. Power Automate generates the summary. Logs it into an excel sheet. One candidate ID lookup = one final rec → STRONG HIRE or no. Rooted in real numbers.
>
> If we had more time, we'd add OCR parsing for resumes instead of raw text input. Trigger the whole flow the moment a calendar slot gets booked. And build in an auditing layer to catch bias and hallucinations before they reach a hiring recommendation.
>
> 1,440 minutes. Straight grind 🚀

- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7454707907176206336/

> 💻 Winner in Finance & Accounting Track. Gies AI for Impact Challenge in 24 hours.
>
> Last Friday, during the 24-hour Gies AI for Impact Challenge, our team built **EVA** on Microsoft Copilot Studio.
>
> The project started from a question I was excited to explore: What if innovation budgeting could be handled with the same financial discipline, trigger logic, and auditability as an investment team?
>
> That became EVA: an AI-powered portfolio intelligence advisor designed to help leaders identify which projects to stop, which to scale, and how to redeploy freed capital across strategic scenarios.
>
> The problem we focused on is a real capital allocation challenge: every month, CFOs and innovation leaders need to decide which projects to kill, which to scale, and where to redeploy freed capital. But today, that process is often manual, subjective, and slow. EVA makes that workflow faster, more structured, and auditable.
>
> Our system evaluates 10 innovation projects across four dimensions: Strategic Alignment, Spend Efficiency, Milestone Adherence, and Signal Strength. It also applies override rules inspired by financial covenant triggers, so severe budget and milestone issues are flagged immediately.
>
> What I am most proud of is EVA's scenario-driven capital reallocation logic. Instead of giving one rigid recommendation, EVA frames freed capital across three strategic options:
>
> - 📃 Protect Near-Term Returns
> - 💰 Repair Portfolio Balance
> - 📶 Bet on Strongest Signals
>
> The final product included a Copilot-powered conversational advisor, an automated portfolio review flow, an interactive dashboard, and a leadership briefing generator — reducing a 4-hour manual review process into a 2-minute automated workflow. This project pushed me to think like a CFO, build like a product manager, and move like a founder.
